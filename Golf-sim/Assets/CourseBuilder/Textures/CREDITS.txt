Ground textures, all CC0 1.0 public domain from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com).
Each folder holds <Name>_Color.jpg, <Name>_Normal.jpg (OpenGL) and <Name>_MaskMap.png packed for URP terrain
(G = ambient occlusion, B = displacement, A = 1 - roughness); SOURCE.txt in each folder says the same.
Tools/unity_scripts/SetupTurf.cs and SetupSceneryThemes.cs turn them into TerrainLayers (Assets/CourseBuilder/Layers).
